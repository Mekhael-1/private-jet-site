Client Assurance

Clear protocols for every aspect of your journey.

How fast can an aircraft be airborne for emergency travel?

Through our dedicated operator network with standby crews at key global hubs, we routinely achieve "wheels up" within 3 hours from confirmation and cleared funds.

What are the luggage allowances and pet travel protocols?

Pets are warmly welcomed in the cabin alongside you without crates on virtually all aircraft. Luggage capacity depends on aircraft size, ranging from 8 full golf sets on midsize to over 20 large designer suitcases on the Gulfstream G700.

How is guest discretion and passenger privacy handled?

We maintain strict non-disclosure policies. Passenger manifests are processed via end-to-end encrypted servers. Private FBO terminals allow you to step directly from your chauffeur vehicle to the jet steps without entering a public terminal.

Can catering and in-flight entertainment be customized?

Every charter flight includes tailored catering from premier regional restaurants and top-tier cellars. High-speed Starlink Ka-band Wi-Fi is enabled on our long-range fleet for seamless streaming and conference calls.
